Manhunt 2 Uncut Patcher

Turns your own US copy of Manhunt 2 (PS2) into the Uncut Hybrid ISO. Everything runs on this device; nothing is uploaded. Works offline.

1

Pick your clean US ISO

Manhunt 2 (USA), SLUS-21613, unmodified. Expected: 2.47 GB (2,657,058,816 bytes), CRC32 8f57c95b.

2

Pick the patch file

Manhunt 2 Uncut Hybrid.xdelta (61.7 MB)

3

Patch and save

Saves SLUS_216.13.MANHUNT 2.iso (2.93 GB (3,141,462,016 bytes)). Keep this exact name: OPL on a real PS2 needs it to recognize the game.

Standard xdelta (VCDIFF) patch: if you prefer, Delta Patcher, xdelta UI, UniPatcher (Android) or xdelta3 -d -s also work with the same file.
